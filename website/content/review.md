Saved meetings keep recordings, transcripts and linked notes together. Open a meeting from the library to return to the conversation.

## Replay a meeting

Use the player to play, pause and seek. The **Notes**, **Transcript** and **Ask AI** views provide different ways to work with the same meeting context.

Recordings are saved on the device. If a recording file was moved or deleted outside Noter.ai, playback may be unavailable until the file or its backup is restored.

## Search the transcript

Open **Transcript**, search for a phrase, and select a passage to seek to that point. Speaker filters can narrow the displayed passages.

Enable **Follow playback** to keep the active passage in view as the player moves. Turn it off when you want to browse independently. Some older transcript-only entries have approximate timing; an approximate timestamp is not the same as word-level alignment.

## Review and save the notes

Check the organized note draft against the source material. Add missing details, correct inaccurate wording and confirm owners or deadlines before choosing **Save note** or **Update note**.

Moving a linked note through the app also keeps its associated recording and transcript together. Relinking or detaching a note clears its previous meeting association.

## Ask AI

Ask a specific question about the meeting, such as:

- What decisions were made about the website?
- Which tasks have an explicit owner or deadline?
- What requirements changed during the call?

Answers use the available meeting context and your configured AI provider. They are displayed progressively. Use **Append to note draft** when you want to review useful output in the editable notes.

AI answers are not a replacement for checking important details against the recording. Missing or unclear source material limits what the model can reliably answer.

## Export a useful moment

Where the recording player offers **Export this video part**, choose the relevant segment and save it to your device. Keep the original recording if you still need the full meeting context.

For missing files, playback issues or a stuck highlight, see [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/).
